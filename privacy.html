Pivo Backup – Privacy Policy

Pivo Backup is a personal tool used only by its owner (waldo22a@gmail.com).

  • It uses only the Google Drive "drive.file" permission, which lets it create and manage the files it uploads. It cannot see any other files in Google Drive.
  • It does not collect, sell, share, or store any personal data anywhere other than the owner's own Google Drive.
  • No data is sent to any third party.
  • Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
